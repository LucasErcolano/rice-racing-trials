## Geometry and use

- **JSAE skidpad:** `tracks/fsaej_skidpad/course_definition.json` records a center-to-center distance of 18.250001 m, inner diameter 14.996957 m, outer 21.499679 m, resulting width 3.251361 m; 16 inner and 13 outer cones per circle. It declares a 3 m *virtual* lane width because the mod has no entry/exit cones. Right/right/left/left procedure: second and fourth laps timed. `commissioning/tracks/skidpad_compliance.json` reports seven of twelve D.10 checks deviating; do not call it 2027-compliant.
- **JSAE endurance as autocross:** measured path definition in `tracks/fsaej_endurance/course_definition.json`; `commissioning/tracks/autocross_compliance.json` records 940.525390 m and a minimum measured width of 0.889 m, subject to the analyzer methodology (it does not imply the whole pavement has that width). One timed lap from the grid; a three-lap Assetto configuration keeps the session alive after crossing the finish. The selection report records six of twelve D.11 checks deviating. It is not the Formula SAE Michigan 2027 course nor an officially reproduced autocross; it is an evaluation proxy.

## Reuse and limits

`tools/trackgen.py`, `tools/kn5.py`, `tools/course_maps.py`, `analysis/track_geometry.py`, `tools/validate_fsae_2027_tracks.py` and the JSON definitions allow studying, generating and checking the **Rice fallbacks**; `docs/*COURSE_MAP*` and `commissioning/public_tracks/audit/maps/` serve for visual review. `config/track_lock.json` and `config/FROZEN_TRIAL_CONFIGURATION.json` are relevant for preflight; any substitution requires a new configuration and validation, not renaming a folder. The official folders, their mod files, the MFT02 car and Assetto are not in the ZIP. The claims about JSAE provenance, Windows commissioning, layout fidelity and rules compliance were taken from internal documents; they were not checked against a CAD/GPS survey, original external files or a simulator run. FFB, human controls, grip coefficients and vehicle dynamics were not validated either. `official_enabled=false`: this inventory does not enable official trials.
